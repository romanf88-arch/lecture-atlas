Talk, and the world keeps up.

Say anything you would say in a lecture. Each person, place, work, and idea you mention is found, pictured, set down on the Earth where it belongs, and placed in time. Lines join the ones that are connected.

In Chrome and Edge, listening uses the browser's own speech recognition, which sends audio to the browser maker. In other browsers, such as Brave and Firefox, it uses a speech model that runs inside this page, so the audio stays on your computer. That model is less accurate with unusual names. Names are looked up on Wikipedia and Wikidata. Nothing is saved. Drag the globe to turn it. Click any portrait or card to open it. Text and pictures come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons under their own licenses. Dates and places come from Wikidata. Borders come from Natural Earth.
